About India

The 7th largest nation by land area and the most populous country in the world — a vibrant mosaic of landscapes, ecosystems, and ancient national symbols.

Physical Features

Geography & Landscapes

Stretching from the 8,000-meter peaks of the northern Himalayas to the tropical coconut palms of Kanyakumari.

🏔️ The Himalayan Wall

Forming the northern boundary, the Himalayas act as a natural climatic barrier protecting the subcontinent while nurturing major sacred rivers like the Ganges, Indus, and Brahmaputra.

🏜️ The Great Thar Desert

Spanning Rajasthan, the Thar Desert features rolling sand dunes, vibrant folk arts, and historic fortresses built into golden sandstone mountains.

🌴 Coastal Ghats & Backwaters

Flanked by the Western and Eastern Ghats mountain ranges, India features over 7,500 km of coastline bordering the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal.

Six Distinct Regions

Select a region to learn about its unique climate, culture, and key states.

North India

Key States: Delhi, Punjab, Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh.

Home to the Taj Mahal, Golden Temple, Himalayan hill stations, and royal Mughlai cuisine.

South India

Key States: Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh.

Renowned for Dravidian temples, Ayurveda, coconut-infused curries, tech hubs like Bengaluru, and Alleppey backwaters.

East India

Key States: West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand.

Famous for literary heritage, Victoria Memorial in Kolkata, Konark Sun Temple, and mouthwatering sweets like Rasgulla.

West India

Key States: Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Rajasthan.

Home to Mumbai (the financial & Bollywood capital), sun-kissed beaches of Goa, and majestic palaces of Jaipur.

North-East India

Key States: Assam, Meghalaya, Sikkim, Nagaland, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura.

Known as the 'Seven Sisters', featuring living root bridges, tea gardens of Assam, and pristine Himalayan valleys.

Identity & Heritage

National Symbols of India

Representing the sovereignty, pride, and spiritual consciousness of the nation.

National Animal

Royal Bengal Tiger

Symbolizing grace, power, and agility. India is home to Project Tiger, safeguarding over 3,000 wild tigers across 54 reserves.

National Flower

The Sacred Lotus

Representing purity, knowledge, and spiritual enlightenment as it blooms untouched above muddy waters.

State Emblem

Lion Capital of Ashoka

Adapted from the Emperor Ashoka pillar at Sarnath, featuring four lions standing back-to-back above the Dharma Chakra wheel with the motto Satyameva Jayate (Truth Alone Triumphs).
