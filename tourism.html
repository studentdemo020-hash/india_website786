Iconic Travel Experiences

Click any destination card to open deep-dive details and travel tips.

Agra • Heritage

Taj Mahal & Mughal Forts

Agra, Uttar Pradesh. Marvel at the world's grandest marble monument of eternal love and explore Akbar's imperial Red Fort.

Kerala • Nature

Alleppey Houseboat Backwaters

Alleppey, Kerala. Drift lazily along palm-shaded lagoons on a luxury hand-crafted houseboat with authentic banana-leaf lunches.

Karnataka • Ruins

Hampi & Madurai Stone Temples

Karnataka & Tamil Nadu. Wander through surreal boulder landscapes, chariot stone sculptures, and soaring rainbow temple gopurams.

Rajasthan • Wildlife

Ranthambore Tiger Safari

Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan. Embark on open-air jeep safaris through ancient dry-deciduous forests to spot wild Royal Bengal Tigers.

Varanasi • Spiritual

Sacred Ghats of Varanasi

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Witness dusk Ganga Aarti ceremonies with brass oil lamps, incense, and floating flower offerings along the holy river.

Delhi & Jaipur • Culinary

Delhi & Jaipur Street Food Tours

Delhi, Rajasthan, Kochi. Walk through centuries-old spice markets in Chandni Chowk and taste royal thali banquets in Pink City palaces.
