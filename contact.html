Frequently Asked Questions

Essential advice for travelers visiting India for the first time.

The peak travel season is from October to March when the weather across most of the country is pleasantly cool and dry. Monsoon season runs from June to September.
Yes, most travelers require a visa. India offers a convenient electronic visa (e-Visa) for citizens of over 160 countries, which can be easily applied for online prior to arrival.
India is the world's most vegetarian-friendly nation! Over 30-40% of the population is strictly vegetarian, and all packaged food is clearly marked with a green dot symbol for vegetarian items.
Indian Railways connects virtually every corner of the nation and offers a scenic, memorable journey. High-speed Vande Bharat express trains and domestic flights are ideal for longer distances.
