📜 Five Millennia of Tapestry

History & Culture

From ancient urban planning of Mohenjo-Daro to golden imperial dynasties, spiritual philosophies, and classical performing arts that resonate across generations.

Interactive Chronology

Timeline of Key Historical Eras

Filter by era to explore the events that shaped Indian civilization.

c. 3300 – 1300 BCE

Indus Valley Civilization

One of the world's earliest urban civilizations (Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro), famous for sophisticated grid town planning, baked brick houses, drainage systems, and metallurgy.

c. 322 – 185 BCE

The Maurya Empire & Emperor Ashoka

Chandragupta Maurya unified most of the subcontinent. Emperor Ashoka converted to Buddhism, championing non-violence (Ahimsa) and spreading edicts across stone pillars.

c. 319 – 543 CE

Gupta Empire — Golden Age of Science & Arts

Known as the Golden Age of India. Aryabhata invented the concept of zero and calculated pi; Kalidasa authored classic Sanskrit poetry; Ajanta cave paintings were created.

c. 1336 – 1646 CE

Vijayanagara Empire & Chola Naval Power

Southern India flourished with monumental granite temple architecture in Hampi, while Chola maritime expeditions extended Indian influence across Southeast Asia.

1526 – 1857 CE

Mughal Empire & Monumental Wonders

Emperors Akbar and Shah Jahan presided over an era of Indo-Islamic architectural marvels, constructing the Taj Mahal, Red Fort Agra, and Fatehpur Sikri.

1857 – 1947 CE

Freedom Struggle & Non-Violent Movement

Mahatma Gandhi led millions in peaceful Satyagraha (civil disobedience) and Dandi Salt March, inspiring global civil rights movements.

August 15, 1947 – Present

Independence & Sovereign Democratic Republic

Enacted the world's longest written Constitution on January 26, 1950. Today, India is a global economic power and leader in space exploration (Chandrayaan & Mangalyaan).

Living Traditions

Classical Arts & Major Festivals

Celebrating expression, devotion, and community harmony.

Festival

Diwali & Holi

Diwali celebrates the triumph of light over darkness with clay lamps and fireworks. Holi marks spring with joyous bursts of vibrant colored powders (gulal).

Performing Arts

8 Classical Dance Forms

From Bharatanatyam (Tamil Nadu) and Kathak (North India) to Kathakali (Kerala) — intricate mudras (hand gestures) tell ancient mythological epics.

Architecture

Sacred & Royal Architecture

Dravidian rock-cut temples, Rajput fortresses perched on cliffs, and symmetrical Mughal gardens showcase extraordinary craftsmanship.
